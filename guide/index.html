Getting the most out of Heracles Records

From the first launch to a second computer, and what to do when something is not right.

  • Getting started

    Install the app, sign in with COROS and find your way around in ten minutes.

  • Connect your COROS account

    What signing in reads, where it is kept, and why COROS may ask you to sign in again.

  • Set up Coach

    Connect the AI Coach runs on, choose what each conversation may read, and set its tone.

  • Sleep and health data

    Connect COROS MCP once to bring in nights, naps, HRV and stress, and keep them past COROS's nine weeks.

  • Strength and Hevy

    Bring your Hevy workouts in beside your COROS strength sessions.

    Keep conversations, plans and settings the same on every computer, through your own Google Drive.

    Save everything to one file you keep, and bring it back on this computer or another.
