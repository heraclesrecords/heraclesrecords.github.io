Strength, counted properly.

COROS scores lifting by heart rate, so an hour of heavy squats can count for less than a short jog. Heracles Records scores every set from the weight, the reps and how close to failure it went, on the same scale as your runs.

How the formula works

A mentor that proposes, never decides.

It runs on the AI you already have: your ChatGPT account, a Claude subscription, an API key or a model on your own computer. Changes to your week arrive as a list you apply one line at a time.

How Mentor works

Your training stays yours.

The app has no account of its own and the developer runs no servers. Sync two computers through your own Google Drive, or keep everything in one backup file.
