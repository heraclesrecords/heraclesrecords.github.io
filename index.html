What it does

  • Training

    Every session COROS holds, with a screen each for running, cycling, hiking and strength, plus sleep, recovery and every place you have trained.

  • Planning

    Your COROS workouts, plans and calendar. Build, edit and schedule them here, and they are saved straight to your COROS account, so your watch has them.

  • Coach

    An AI coach that has read your training. It answers with the numbers and charts behind them, drafts workouts and plans, and changes nothing without you.

  • Hall of Records

    Every first, record and streak, month by month, and the Twelve Labours: twelve kinds of achievement in three stages each.

  • Your data

    Kept on your computer. Sync two computers through your own Google Drive, or back everything up to one file you keep.

Heracles Records reads your training through your own COROS account, so it works with any COROS watch that syncs there. It has no account of its own and its developer runs no servers. Privacy policy
