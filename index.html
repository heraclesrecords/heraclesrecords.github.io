Coach

A coach that has read your training.

Ask about a session, a week or how well you are recovering. Coach reads your COROS data and answers with the numbers and the charts behind them.

  • Plans with you. The brief, the outline, then every session, saved to COROS and your calendar when you are happy with it.
  • Proposes, never decides. Changes to your week arrive as a list you apply one line at a time.
  • Your AI, your choice. ChatGPT, Claude, OpenRouter or a model on your own computer.
  • Reads only what you allow. Each conversation has its own sources, and Coach sees nothing it is not given.

Auto analysis: Coach checks in on its own.

Give a conversation a standing job and Coach does it without being asked: after a new activity, on a schedule, or when something in your data turns. The answer lands in the same conversation, ready when you open it.

  • After each activity Post-activity debrief How the session went against your recent training, and anything worth acting on.
  • Every morning Morning briefing What today should look like, given the last few days and what is on the calendar.
  • Every Sunday Weekly review The week as a whole: what held, what slipped and what it means for the next one.
  • Every Monday Next week's plan The week's sessions drafted from recent training, waiting for your approval.

Read-only by design: an analysis can read and suggest, never write. Anything it drafts waits for you, and a monthly budget caps what it may spend.

How Coach works

Hall of Records

Twelve labours. Three stages each.

Every first, record and streak is kept, month by month. The Twelve Labours turn them into standards: what a beginner reaches in a few weeks, a few months, a year. Speed and VO₂max are graded for your age and sex, so a stage means the same for everyone.

  • Nemean Lion Strength
  • Hydra Swimming
  • Ceryneian Hind Consistency
  • Erymanthian Boar Mountains
  • Augean Stables Volume
  • Stymphalian Birds Cycling
  • Cretan Bull Long runs
  • Mares of Diomedes Speed
  • Hippolyta Plans
  • Geryon Exploration
  • Hesperides Aerobic fitness
  • Cerberus Sleep

Appearance

Light or dark, and your own colours.

Pick a colour mode and an accent, give each sport its own colour, choose metric or imperial, and keep only the sport screens you train.

Your data

Your training stays yours.

  • On your computer. The app has no account of its own and the developer runs no servers.
  • Two computers, one record. Sync them through your own Google Drive. Passwords and sign-ins never leave the machine they were made on.
  • One file to keep. Back everything up to a file and restore it whenever you like.

Questions

Before you download.

Is it made by COROS?
No. Heracles Records is an independent app. It reads your training through your own COROS account, the same way the COROS Training Hub website does.
Does it cost anything?
The app is free. Coach runs on an AI you choose: your own ChatGPT or Claude plan, a pay-as-you-go API key, or a free model on your own computer.
Can I use it without the AI coach?
Yes. Every screen but Coach works without one.
Where is my data?
On your computer. Nothing goes to the developer. If you sync two computers, it goes through your own Google Drive.
