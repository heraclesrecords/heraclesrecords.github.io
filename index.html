Heracles Records

A desktop companion for COROS athletes.

Heracles Records is a free, open-source app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Sign in with your COROS account to read your training in depth, plan it with an AI coach, and keep your watch stocked with music. Everything runs on your own computer.

Download Source code Privacy policy

What it does

  • Training — every activity, with screens for running, cycling, hiking and strength, plus sleep, recovery and the places you have trained.
  • Planning — your COROS workouts, plans and calendar, saved straight to your COROS account.
  • Coach — an AI coach that reads your training data and drafts workouts and plans, using the AI account or key you choose.
  • Music — tracks from YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and podcasts, converted and copied to your watch over USB.
  • Sync — optionally keeps your conversations, plans and preferences the same on each of your computers, through a folder in your own Google Drive.
